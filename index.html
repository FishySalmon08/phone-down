Phone Down Challenge

Pick your group, then reveal your secret card. Keep it to yourself.

1Your group

How scoring works
  • Blue: your group loses 1 point every time you use your phone. Make it to the end without touching it and your group gets +2 points.
  • Red: you are never punished yourself. But every time you use your phone, your group loses 1 point, and you take a small piece of candy from the prize pot.
  • Expect messages in the group chat trying to tempt you.
  • The group with the most points wins whatever candy is left in the pot.
